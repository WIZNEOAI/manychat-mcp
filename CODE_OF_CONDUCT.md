# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want a focused, respectful community where contributors can discuss technical tradeoffs without harassment or personal attacks.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific when reviewing ideas and code.
- Critique technical decisions, not people.
- Make space for different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Protect private data and credentials shared during responsible security work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, doxxing, repeated unwanted contact, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub's contact or reporting tools. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when needed. They will handle reports with discretion and avoid sharing private details beyond those who need them to respond.
